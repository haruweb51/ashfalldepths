ASHFALL DEPTHS

Descend. Endure. Parry.

Beat The Hollow King to free Ashfall.......
New Game offers an optional tutorial with the guide Choiatsm (a flat training world: movement, combat, items) · skip it any time
A/D move · Space jump (again in air = double jump) · W/S climb ladders · Shift dodge · LMB attack
RMB block (tap right before a hit = parry)
Four layers, top to bottom: Ember · Glacial · Tempest · Hollow — each sealed by a gate (glowing portal)
Each layer's boss waits in an arena at its far east end (follow the corridor right from the layer's last gate chamber)
2 lives · level up to regain one · lose them all and the run ends — you bank echoes based on your level (each level is worth more than the last) to spend on permanent upgrades
Every new run lets you pick Echo Modifiers (harder rules, more echoes) · badges for slain bosses and conquered modifiers show below
Dash has a 0.2s cooldown · a broken guard stuns you for 1.5s · sell spare gear to the Merchant for 15% of its price · chests refill after ~5 min
R drink Health Elixir (45 HP, 5s cooldown, 200g each at the Merchant — upgradable) · Z / X cast equipped spells (buy at the Merchant)
E mine block / talk to the Merchant, the Questwarden or the Enchanting Altar at spawn (500g per enchant reroll) / use a Shrine to teleport (Spawn + each unlocked layer) and rekindle defeated bosses (+10% stats & rewards each time) · Tab character · Esc pause · M mute
